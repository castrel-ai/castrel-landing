## New Feature: Alert Workbench

When managing multiple monitoring systems, operations teams often face alerts scattered across different platforms. Troubleshooting demands manual aggregation and guesswork about correlations — a fragmented process that slows everything down.

The Alert Workbench brings alerts from different sources together into a single processing interface. Incoming alerts from Prometheus, Zabbix, and other monitoring sources are deduplicated and aggregated through default or customizable compression rules, producing structured alerts that keep critical signals from being buried in noise.

When deeper investigation is needed, you can turn one or more related alerts into an incident and bind it to the relevant service or application — making the incident the organizing unit for your troubleshooting effort. Once created, associated alerts can be flexibly added or removed, and every step of the investigation revolves around the incident with full context at hand.
