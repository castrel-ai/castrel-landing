## New Feature: Alert Workbench

When managing multiple monitoring systems, operations teams often face alerts scattered across different platforms. Troubleshooting demands manual aggregation and guesswork about correlations — a fragmented process that slows everything down.

The Alert Workbench brings alerts from different sources together into a single processing interface. Taking Prometheus as an example, the integration page includes a built-in toggle that lets you start or stop alert syncing with a single click — turn it on and alerts flow into the workbench automatically, turn it off and pulling stops right away, no need to touch connector settings.

![Prometheus Alert Pull Toggle](/images/changelog/v1-21-0/switch-en.gif)

Once alerts from Prometheus, Zabbix, and other monitoring sources enter the workbench, they are deduplicated and aggregated through default or customizable compression rules, producing structured alerts that keep critical signals from being buried in noise.

When deeper investigation is needed, you can turn one or more related alerts into an incident and bind it to the relevant service or application — making the incident the organizing unit for your troubleshooting effort. Once created, associated alerts can be flexibly added or removed, and every step of the investigation revolves around the incident with full context at hand.
