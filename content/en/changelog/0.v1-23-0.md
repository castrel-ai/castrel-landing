## New Feature: Model Switching on the Conversation Home

When starting a conversation, you can choose the most suitable model and thinking intensity for your task. Different models balance response speed and reasoning depth differently, so you can switch as needed to get quick results for lightweight tasks or more thorough reasoning for complex analysis.

The conversation home offers DeepSeek V4 Flash, DeepSeek V4 Pro, Qwen 3.5 Plus, and Qwen 3.7 Plus, with more models to be added over time to cover a wider range of scenarios.

## Full Release Checklist

### feat

- Added the ability to choose the right AI model when starting a conversation, for faster replies or deeper reasoning.
- Added WeCom support for creating groups, viewing group and member details, and sending direct or group messages.
- Added a Custom Alerts feature for creating webhooks to receive custom alerts.
- Added alert integration for monitoring platforms like Datadog, Grafana, AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Coralogix, and Loki.
- Added reliable storage for chat attachments, documents, and knowledge outputs without affecting existing content.

### bugfix

- Fixed an issue where mentioning the bot in a group chat produced duplicate replies after a session was created.
- Fixed an issue where clicking a knowledge reference at the end of a conversation showed the tool execution details panel instead of the knowledge content.
