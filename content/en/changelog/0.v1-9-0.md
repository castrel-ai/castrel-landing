## New Feature: Task Archive & Deletion

We've introduced full archive and deletion capabilities for task lifecycle management, keeping your workspace clean and focused:

- **Quick archive from sidebar:** Hovering over a task reveals an archive button on the right side. A confirmation click completes the archival — smooth and non-disruptive to your workflow.
- **Expanded action menu:** The task action menu now includes "Archive Task" and "Delete Task" options. Archiving takes effect immediately, while deletion requires a confirmation step to prevent accidental removal.
- **Archived task management panel:** A new "Archived Tasks" tab in Settings displays all archived tasks in a list view, with one-click restoration.
- **Global isolation:** Archived and deleted tasks are hidden across all views — sidebar task list, automation task list, shared links, and more — ensuring your workspace stays focused on active tasks.
