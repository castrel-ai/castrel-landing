## Highlights

- Refactored the knowledge system to a document-first model with better smart-trigger and duplicate-name validation.
- Improved context and discovery workflows with stronger service/infrastructure CRUD and table handling.
- Brought plugin-market and Castrel Proxy release paths into the mainstream product flow.

## Integrations and Platform

- Expanded connectors: Qingfanyun, PagerDuty, Volcengine, Tencent Cloud, Huawei Cloud, and Alibaba Cloud.
- Added DingTalk as an integration option.
- Added membership and credit-allocation capabilities in admin flows.
