## New Feature: Topology Knowledge

Castrel can now generate topology knowledge from connected data sources such as APM and CMDB, combining services, infrastructure, and their dependency and deployment relationships into one system topology. You get a full view of the current environment without mapping it by hand.

A generated topology is editable — add nodes and adjust relationships so it matches your real architecture. The topology stays available as shared context for later operations, helping you understand service dependencies and impact when handling alerts, investigating incidents, or running routine checks. See the [Incident Investigation documentation](/docs/features/incident-investigation) for how it's used during an investigation.
